## Concerts, Musicals & Arts Exhibitions

### Dirty Dancing: The Musical
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 28 · 2:00 PM (sold out) and 7:30 PM; Sunday Nov 29 · 2:00 PM
- **Where:** CAA Ed Mirvish Theatre, 244 Victoria St
- **Cost:** Ticket prices vary; the Saturday matinee is sold out
- **Info:** The North American tour of the stage version of the 1987 film settles into its Toronto run (Nov 24 – Jan 10), and this is its first weekend. The Kellerman's story comes with the film's songs — "(I've Had) The Time of My Life", "Hungry Eyes", "Do You Love Me?" — and a dance-heavy finale. 2 hours 30 minutes including intermission; recommended ages 12+, with haze, strobe and loud thunder effects.
- **Tags:** musical, dance, indoor
- **Link:** [Dirty Dancing at Mirvish](https://www.mirvish.com/shows/dirty-dancing)

### Operation Mincemeat: A New Musical
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 28 · 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM; Sunday Nov 29 · 2:00 PM
- **Where:** Royal Alexandra Theatre, 260 King St W
- **Cost:** Ticket prices vary
- **Info:** The Olivier winner for Best New Musical and a 2025 Tony winner, in its Canadian premiere through Dec 13. The true Second World War caper — a stolen corpse, a fake love letter and Ian Fleming — told as a fast five-person ensemble piece. 2 hours 35 minutes including intermission; recommended ages 5+, with strobe, darkness and loud noises.
- **Tags:** musical, comedy, canadian premiere, indoor
- **Link:** [Operation Mincemeat at Mirvish](https://www.mirvish.com/shows/operation-mincemeat-a-new-musical)

### AGO — Sunday Best: The Power & The Glory of Black Style
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 28 & Sunday Nov 29 · 10:30 AM–5:30 PM
- **Where:** Art Gallery of Ontario, 317 Dundas St W
- **Cost:** Included with general admission ($30 adult; free for Ontarians under 25)
- **Info:** A world premiere made with the Philadelphia Museum of Art, tracing Black style from the 1880s to now through fashion, visual art, ephemera and time-based media — migration, activism, self-determination and the ritual of dressing in one's best. Runs to Feb 28, 2027.

## Food, Festivals & Special Experiences

### Cavalcade of Lights — Opening Night
- **Day:** Saturday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 28 · 6:00–11:00 PM (tree-lighting countdown at 6:30 PM)
- **Where:** Nathan Phillips Square, 100 Queen St W
- **Cost:** Free
- **Info:** The city's holiday season switches on, and the square stays lit nightly to Jan 7. Opening night has the countdown to light Toronto's official Christmas tree, public skating on the rink (bring skates or rent on site), drop-in lantern workshops with Shadowland Theatre, stilt walkers and a Jack Frost juggler from Hala Circus Arts, main-stage sets from Mohawk pop/R&B artist Semiah and reggae-dancehall artist Ammoye, an 8 PM lantern parade with marching band Gayly Forward, ice performances by Canada's Drag Race's Star Doll and Carmen Sutra, and two Fire in Motion shows. Two illuminated installations, ECHO and Ripples in Time, glow over the square each evening.
- **Tags:** festival, holiday, free, outdoor
- **Link:** [Cavalcade of Lights](https://www.toronto.ca/explore-enjoy/festivals-events/cavalcade-of-lights)

### One of a Kind Christmas Show
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 28 · 10:00 AM–8:00 PM; Sunday Nov 29 · 10:00 AM–5:00 PM
- **Where:** Enercare Centre, Exhibition Place, 100 Princes' Blvd
- **Cost:** Adult $23 online ($25.50 at the door); children 12 and under free
- **Info:** The Christmas edition of the country's big craft and design fair, Nov 27 – Dec 6: 800-plus Canadian makers across ceramics, jewellery, textiles, wood and food, 175 of them new this year, plus stage programming, fashion shows and hands-on workshops. - **Tags:** market, craft, holiday, indoor
- **Link:** [One of a Kind Christmas Show](https://oneofakindshow.com/home/)
