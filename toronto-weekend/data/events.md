## Concerts, Musicals & Arts Exhibitions

### The Karate Kid – The Musical
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Oct 31 · 2:00 PM; Sunday Nov 1 · 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM (final performance)
- **Where:** Princess of Wales Theatre, 300 King St W
- **Cost:** Ticket prices vary; closing weekend
- **Info:** The stage adaptation of the 1984 film ends its Toronto run on Sunday. Both Mirvish houses go dark on Halloween night, so Saturday's only performance is the 2:00 PM matinee, and a 7:30 PM show was added on Sunday for the last night. Runs 2 hours 30 minutes including intermission.
- **Tags:** musical, theatre, film adaptation, closing weekend, indoor
- **Link:** [The Karate Kid at Mirvish](https://www.mirvish.com/shows/the-karate-kid)

### Operation Mincemeat: A New Musical
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Oct 31 · 2:00 PM; Sunday Nov 1 · 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM
- **Where:** Royal Alexandra Theatre, 260 King St W
- **Cost:** Ticket prices vary
- **Info:** The Olivier Award winner for Best New Musical and a 2025 Tony winner, in its Canadian premiere — the true Second World War story of a stolen corpse, a fake love letter and Ian Fleming, told as a fast, funny ensemble piece. Runs 2 hours 35 minutes including intermission, until Dec 13.
- **Tags:** musical, comedy, canadian premiere, indoor
- **Link:** [Operation Mincemeat at Mirvish](https://www.mirvish.com/shows/operation-mincemeat-a-new-musical)

### AGO — Sunday Best: The Power & The Glory of Black Style
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Oct 31 & Sunday Nov 1 · 10:30 AM–5:30 PM

## Food, Festivals & Special Experiences

### Art Toronto 2026
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Oct 31 · 12:00–7:00 PM; Sunday Nov 1 · 12:00–6:00 PM (final day)
- **Where:** Metro Toronto Convention Centre, North Building, 255 Front St W
- **Cost:** Adult $40; Friday–Sunday fair pass $60; Night Out after 6:00 PM $30
- **Info:** Canada's international art fair puts more than 100 galleries, solo presentations and installations in the north hall. The closing weekend is the busiest, and the Saturday-evening Night Out ticket is the cheap way in.
- **Tags:** art fair, galleries, design, indoor
- **Link:** [Art Toronto 2026](https://www.arttoronto.ca/)

### Halloween on Church 2026
- **Day:** Saturday
- **When:** Saturday Oct 31 · street party from 5:00 PM; Witches' Brew beer garden and mega stage 5:00 PM–midnight
- **Where:** Church St between Gloucester and Carlton; beer garden at Church & Maitland
- **Cost:** Free to walk the street; beer garden is 19+ with photo ID
- **Info:** Six blocks of the Village close to traffic for two nights of costumed crowds — drag and tribute acts on the mega stage (Sofonda, BOA, a Michael Jackson tribute), go-go dancers and a runway show, with bars open late. Costumes encouraged; the street is pedestrian-only, so take the subway.
- **Tags:** halloween, street festival, drag, free, outdoor
- **Link:** [Halloween on Church](https://www.halloweenonchurch.ca/)

### Legends of Horror at Casa Loma
- **Day:** Saturday
- **When:** Saturday Oct 31 · Family Hours 5:00–6:30 PM; Standard Hours from 7:00 PM (final night of the season)
- **Where:** Casa Loma, 1 Austin Terrace (entrance at Davenport & Walmer Rd)
- **Cost:** Family Hours $45 online / $50 onsite; Standard Hours $55 online / $60 onsite; VIP package for two $200
- **Info:** A self-guided 2 km trail through the lower gardens, underground tunnels and rarely opened chambers, with live characters, a mid-way bar with a DJ and 3D projection mapping across the castle facade to finish. Family Hours run earlier with no live actors. Cashless, and not step-free.
- **Tags:** halloween, immersive, casa loma, night out
- **Link:** [Legends of Horror at Casa Loma](https://legendsofhorror.ca/)

### Day of the Dead Toronto
- **Day:** Sunday
- **When:** Sunday Nov 1 · 10:00 AM–8:00 PM
- **Where:** Nathan Phillips Square, 100 Queen St W
- **Cost:** Free
- **Info:** Traditional ofrendas, live music and dance, a Calaveritas pavilion with workshops, pan de muerto and Mexican food, and a mercadito of local artisans — the city's main Día de Muertos gathering, and a calm counterpoint to Halloween.
- **Tags:** festival, día de muertos, free, outdoor
- **Link:** [Day of the Dead Toronto](https://dayofthedeadtoronto.com/)
