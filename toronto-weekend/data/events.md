### TSO — Mendelssohn's Scottish
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 21 · 7:30 PM; Sunday Nov 22 · 3:00 PM
- **Where:** Roy Thomson Hall, 60 Simcoe St (Sat); George Weston Recital Hall, 5040 Yonge St (Sun)
- **Cost:** Ticket prices vary
- **Info:** French violinist Renaud Capuçon both plays and conducts: Charlotte Sohy's rarely heard Thème varié, Schumann's Violin Concerto — left unperformed for more than 80 years after his death — and Mendelssohn's Third, the "Scottish". Note the Sunday matinee moves out of Roy Thomson Hall to George Weston Recital Hall in North York.
- **Tags:** orchestra, classical, indoor
- **Link:** [Mendelssohn's Scottish at the TSO](https://www.tso.ca/concerts-and-events/events/mendelssohns-scottish)

## Food, Festivals & Special Experiences

### The Original Santa Claus Parade
- **Day:** Sunday
- **When:** Sunday Nov 22 · 12:30 PM start
- **Where:** Starts at Christie Pits (Christie St & Bloor St W), then east on Bloor, south on Yonge St, west on Richmond, south on University, east on Wellington and south on Bay to Front St, finishing near St. Lawrence Market
- **Cost:** Free
- **Info:** The 122nd edition, and the first in more than a decade to come down Yonge Street — downtown construction pushed the southbound leg off University. The route runs about 7 km past the Eaton Centre and Sankofa Square, a new activation zone opening at 11:00 AM. First floats reach St. Lawrence Market around 2:15–2:30 PM and the parade wraps by 4:00–4:30 PM; Santa's Treats & Twinkles zone at Christie Pits opens at 10:00 AM. Free, and broadcast live on Citytv.
- **Tags:** parade, holiday, free, outdoor
- **Link:** [The Original Santa Claus Parade](https://www.thesantaclausparade.com/the-parade)

### HarbourFrost — Opening Weekend & Makers Market
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 21 · 12:00–9:00 PM; Sunday Nov 22 · 12:00–6:00 PM (Makers Market 12:00–6:00 PM both days)
- **Where:** Harbourfront Centre, 235 Queens Quay W
- **Cost:** Free, no tickets required
- **Info:** The first weekend of HarbourFrost, Harbourfront's new winter festival (Nov 20 – Dec 20), anchored by a Makers Market that runs only Friday to Sunday. Ceramics, glass, textiles and jewellery from the centre's Craft & Design artists-in-residence, with the studios open so you can watch the work being made. Also free on site: the Frostbite Lane vendors, the Redpath Express train, Hala Circus stilt-walking characters and Illuminair's fire performances.
- **Tags:** festival, market, holiday, waterfront
- **Link:** [HarbourFrost Makers Market](https://harbourfrontcentre.com/event/harbourfrost-makers-market-2026/)
