### AGO — The Impressionist Revolution: Monet to Matisse
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** On now until Oct 18, 2026
- **Where:** Art Gallery of Ontario, 317 Dundas St W
- **Cost:** Special exhibition ticket
- **Info:** Why it's top-tier: this is one of Toronto's rare blockbuster loan exhibitions this year — major Impressionist and Post-Impressionist works (Monet to Matisse) borrowed from the Dallas Museum of Art. You don't often get this calibre of European masterpieces in a single AGO show.
- **Tags:** AGO, impressionism, Monet, Matisse, special exhibition
- **Link:** [AGO Exhibition](https://ago.ca/exhibitions/impressionist-revolution-monet-matisse-dallas-museum-art)

### Aga Khan Museum — Hassan Hajjaj: La Salle de Gym des Femmes Arabes
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** On now until Sep 7, 2026
- **Where:** Aga Khan Museum, 77 Wynford Dr
- **Cost:** Museum admission
- **Info:** Why it's top-tier: Hassan Hajjaj is one of the most internationally recognized contemporary photographers working today, and this installation reimagines an Arab women's gym as a vibrant immersive environment. It closes Sep 7, and this is one of the most distinctive museum experiences currently in Toronto.
- **Tags:** Aga Khan Museum, photography, installation, closing soon
- **Link:** [Hassan Hajjaj Exhibition](https://agakhanmuseum.org/whats-on/hassan-hajjaj/)

### Gardiner Museum — Highways of Harlan House
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** On now until Sep 13, 2026
- **Where:** Gardiner Museum, 111 Queen's Park
- **Cost:** Museum admission
- **Info:** Why it's top-tier: this is the first time in over 50 years that Harlan House's early wall sculptures have been shown publicly. It's a rare rediscovery of an important Canadian studio pottery artist, making it the standout ceramics special exhibition of the year in Toronto.
- **Tags:** Gardiner Museum, ceramics, special exhibition
- **Link:** [Highways of Harlan House](https://www.gardinermuseum.com/highways-of-harlan-house)
