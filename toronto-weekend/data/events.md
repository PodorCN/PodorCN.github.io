### ROM — Stolen Childhoods
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Oct 24 & Sunday Oct 25 · 10:00 AM–5:30 PM (closes Oct 29)
- **Where:** Royal Ontario Museum, 100 Queen's Park (Level 1, Philosopher's Gallery)
- **Cost:** Included with general admission
