## Concerts, Musicals & Arts Exhibitions

### Dr. Seuss' How the Grinch Stole Christmas! The Musical
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 14 · 10:30 AM, 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM; Sunday Nov 15 · 2:00 PM
- **Where:** CAA Ed Mirvish Theatre, 244 Victoria St
- **Cost:** Ticket prices vary
- **Info:** The Broadway holiday staging is in town for a short run (Nov 10–22), with Max the Dog narrating and Seuss-inspired sets and costumes throughout. 85 minutes, no intermission, and the 10:30 AM slot is a Saturday-only added performance.
- **Tags:** musical, holiday, family, indoor
- **Link:** [How the Grinch Stole Christmas at Mirvish](https://www.mirvish.com/shows/dr-seuss-how-the-grinch-stole-christmas-the-musical)

### Operation Mincemeat: A New Musical
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 14 · 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM; Sunday Nov 15 · 2:00 PM
- **Where:** Royal Alexandra Theatre, 260 King St W
- **Cost:** Ticket prices vary
- **Info:** The Olivier Award winner for Best New Musical and a 2025 Tony winner, in its Canadian premiere — the true Second World War story of a stolen corpse, a fake love letter and Ian Fleming, told as a fast, funny ensemble piece. 2 hours 35 minutes including intermission; runs until Dec 13.
- **Tags:** musical, comedy, canadian premiere, indoor
- **Link:** [Operation Mincemeat at Mirvish](https://www.mirvish.com/shows/operation-mincemeat-a-new-musical)

### Our Little Secret: A True New Musical
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 14 · 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM; Sunday Nov 15 · 2:00 PM
- **Where:** CAA Theatre, 651 Yonge St
- **Cost:** Ticket prices vary
- **Info:** Toronto performer Noam Tomaschoff's true story of discovering he has more than 35 siblings around the world — a sold-out 2023 Toronto Fringe hit that went on to Edinburgh, now a full production directed by Broadway's Adam Pascal. 85 minutes, no intermission; closes Nov 22.
- **Tags:** musical, comedy, off-mirvish, indoor
- **Link:** [Our Little Secret at Mirvish](https://www.mirvish.com/shows/our-little-secret-a-true-new-musical)

### The National Ballet of Canada — Emergence / Silent Screen
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 14 · 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM; Sunday Nov 15 · 2:00 PM
- **Where:** Four Seasons Centre for the Performing Arts, 145 Queen St W
- **Cost:** Tickets from $57
- **Info:** A double bill that opened the company's second programme of the season on Nov 13. Crystal Pite's Emergence — the 2009 commission that swept the Dora dance awards — sets a swarm of dancers in a hive-like world on Owen Belton's soundscape, and Sol León and Paul Lightfoot's Silent Screen layers Philip Glass against an abstract silent film. 1 hour 50 minutes with one intermission.
- **Tags:** ballet, contemporary, classical, indoor
- **Link:** [Emergence / Silent Screen at the National Ballet](https://national.ballet.ca/productions/silent-screen-and-emergence/)

### TSO — Four Last Songs
- **Day:** Saturday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 14 · 7:30 PM
- **Where:** Roy Thomson Hall, 60 Simcoe St
- **Cost:** Ticket prices vary
- **Info:** Gustavo Gimeno conducts, and soprano Rachel Willis-Sørensen sings Strauss's Four Last Songs — his farewell meditation on love, nature and mortality. Mahler's "Nicht zu schnell" in Colin Matthews's orchestration opens; Beethoven's Fourth Symphony closes on a burst of high spirits.
- **Tags:** orchestra, classical, vocal, indoor
- **Link:** [Four Last Songs at the TSO](https://www.tso.ca/concerts-and-events/events/four-last-songs)

### AGO — Sunday Best: The Power & The Glory of Black Style
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 14 · 10:30 AM–9:00 PM; Sunday Nov 15 · 10:30 AM–5:30 PM
- **Where:** Art Gallery of Ontario, 317 Dundas St W
- **Cost:** Adult $30; $40 Annual Pass for unlimited visits; free for Ontarians under 25
- **Info:** A world premiere in Toronto before the show travels to the Philadelphia Museum of Art — style and self-fashioning in Black diasporic communities from the 1880s to today, in fashion, photographs, ephemera and film across a full floor. Until Feb 28, 2027.
- **Tags:** ago, fashion, exhibition, indoor
- **Link:** [Sunday Best at the AGO](https://ago.ca/exhibitions/sunday-best-power-glory-black-style)

## Food, Festivals & Special Experiences

### The Royal Agricultural Winter Fair — Final Weekend
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 14 · 9:00 AM–8:00 PM; Sunday Nov 15 · 9:00 AM–6:00 PM (closing weekend)
- **Where:** Exhibition Place — Enercare Centre and Coca-Cola Coliseum, 100 Princes' Blvd
- **Cost:** General admission tickets online; Royal Horse Show and Royal Rodeo tickets are sold separately and include fair admission
- **Info:** The 104th Royal closes on Sunday, and the last two days are the biggest of the run. Saturday's Horse Show has a 1:00 PM K9-Equine Challenge matinee and the 7:00 PM Longines FEI World Cup, the richest purse of the ten days. Sunday is all rodeo: the qualifier at 12:00 PM and the championship finals at 3:00 PM, alongside the butter-sculpture trailer and the Made By Me artisan pavilion.
- **Tags:** fair, food, agriculture, equestrian, rodeo, indoor
- **Link:** [The Royal Agricultural Winter Fair](https://www.royalfair.org/attractions/)

### Distillery Winter Village — Opening Weekend
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 14 & Sunday Nov 15 · free entry before 4:00 PM; tickets required from 4:00 PM
- **Where:** Distillery Historic District, 55 Mill St
- **Cost:** Free before 4 PM; $16 plus fees ($19.89 all-in) after 4 PM; kids 9 and under free
- **Info:** The Christmas market opened Nov 12 with the tree lighting, so this is its first full weekend — the big tree lit over the brick lanes, cabins, carollers, seasonal food and drink, and the separately ticketed Immersive Nutcracker.
- **Tags:** festival, holiday, market, outdoor
- **Link:** [The Distillery Winter Village](https://thedistillerywintervillage.com/)

### Toronto Burlesque Festival — Closing Weekend
- **Day:** Saturday / Sunday
- **When:** Saturday Nov 14 · doors 6:00 PM, show 8:00–10:30 PM; Sunday Nov 15 · doors 12:00 PM, show 2:00–4:00 PM
- **Where:** Buddies in Bad Times Theatre, 12 Alexander St
- **Cost:** From $51.50 standing; $77.20 guaranteed seating; $123.50 VIP (plus fees)
- **Info:** The festival's 2026 edition, Reverie, closes its four-day run with two shows. Saturday's Gilded Glamour pairs a pre-show vendors' market with headliners Jezebel Express and Lou Lou la Duchesse de Rière; Sunday's Through the Looking Glass is a closing brunch with high tea at noon, then a talkback with legend Lovey Goldmine after the show.
- **Tags:** festival, burlesque, performance, indoor
- **Link:** [Toronto Burlesque Festival](https://www.torontoburlesquefestival.ca/)
